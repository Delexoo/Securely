Billing

Billing, cancellation & refunds

Pro is $20 USD per month, shown on the pricing page before checkout. It renews monthly until canceled. Payments are processed by Stripe when checkout is configured.

How to cancel

When the customer portal is enabled, cancel from the billing email Stripe sends you, or contact support via Contact. Until the portal ships, email support to cancel before the next renewal.

Refunds

If you were charged in error, contact us within 14 days of the charge. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe. Chargebacks may suspend the license.

Confirmation

After a successful checkout you should see a confirmation page and receive a Stripe receipt email showing the amount and plan.
