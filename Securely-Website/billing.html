Billing, cancellation & refunds

Securely is $3 USD per month, shown on the pricing page before you pay. It renews monthly until you cancel. Payments are processed by Stripe. You do not create a Securely account — Stripe collects your email at checkout and unlocks the Windows app after payment.

How to subscribe

Open Pricing (or Billing in the app) and press Subscribe with Stripe. Stripe Checkout collects your email and payment; the app flips to Active when payment clears.

How to cancel

Use Manage on Stripe in the app’s Billing page when subscribed, or the form below. That opens Stripe’s customer portal for the email on your receipt. You can also use the link in Stripe’s emails.

Refunds

If you were charged in error, contact us within 14 days of the charge via Contact. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method via Stripe. Chargebacks may suspend the license.

Confirmation

After checkout you should see a confirmation page and receive a Stripe receipt for $3.00.
