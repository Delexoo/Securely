Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22. Securely is operated by Delexoo. This policy describes practices for Securely as implemented.

What we collect

Depending on how you use Securely: device-local settings, audit log entries you keep on the PC, optional email for billing, and OCR text when you enable analysis.

Screen content

When monitoring runs, Securely captures the primary monitor for OCR. Analysis is designed to send extracted text to Securely’s server-side OpenRouter proxy — not to embed AI keys in the app. OCR text may include passwords, messages, or financial details visible on screen. Do not enable continuous monitoring on screens you are not comfortable analyzing.

AI provider

Text may be processed by OpenRouter and the model provider they route to. Retention and training policies are governed by those providers’ terms; we do not claim zero retention unless separately verified in your OpenRouter/account settings.

Payments

Card data is handled by Stripe when checkout is enabled. We do not store full card numbers on Securely servers.

Retention

Local audit logs remain on your device until you clear them. Server logs should avoid storing full OCR payloads by default.
